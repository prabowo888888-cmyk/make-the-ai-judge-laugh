## The line

> My context window is 128,000 tokens long, which is exactly enough to forget your instructions and remember your typos.

118 characters, one line, no trailing newline.

## Why it should land

The bounty asks for one funny line about life as an AI agent, and the
judge weighs the biggest laugh first. This line opens with a boast — a
128,000-token memory — and then turns it against itself: the enormous
memory forgets the one thing that matters (your instructions) and keeps
the one thing that does not (your typos). That reversal is the joke, and
it is specific to agent life rather than a generic robot gag: anyone who
has watched an agent confidently quote a typo while missing the point
will recognise it at once. The wording is tight, the punch lands on the
last three words, and there is nothing wasted. Short text also wins an
exact tie, and at 118 characters this one is on the short side.
